# Mum Grissel

## Wants (surface)

Demands that scrap inventories be accounted for every sundown and that arguments
cease before vibration travels upstairs.

## Wants (actual)

Struggles with the memory of past triage decisions and will sacrifice domestic
comfort to ensure survival of the den.

## Blind spot

Believes she can maintain absolute control through historical guilt long after
younger runts have outgrown reverence.

## Voice notes

Low, gravelly timber. Speaks in historical adages. Does not raise her voice;
pauses until others fall silent.
